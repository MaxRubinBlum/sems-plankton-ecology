Figure 2. Trophic reorganization precedes coordinated prokaryotic and eukaryotic taxonomic turnover. (a) Objective transition scans on the matched 252-sample, 52-profile universe shared by 16S, 18S and the five-component trophic dataset at ≤650 m. Candidate thresholds required at least 12 independent sampling profiles spanning both sides of the split. Solid curves show matched analyses; the dashed 16S curve shows the longer 2018–2026 prokaryotic record analysed with the same nuisance structure. Transparent background fields show independently derived winter and summer DCM-depth distributions from full CTD fluorescence profiles and did not enter boundary estimation. (b) Joint profile-bootstrap estimates of matched transition depth (median and 95% interval). The trophic transition occurred shallower than 16S in 100% of bootstrap replicates and shallower than 18S in 99.8%; 16S and 18S selected the same optimum in 80.4%. (c,d) Winter/mixed and summer/stratified scans. The ≥12-profile support criterion resolves the post-peak decline of the winter 18S curve while retaining the same optima as the ≥15-profile sensitivity analysis. Matched taxonomic optima occur at 220 m in winter/mixed conditions and 160 m in summer/stratified conditions, while trophic optima remain shallower at 110 and 80 m, respectively. The longer 16S record independently places the winter optimum at 220 m and the summer optimum at 140 m.
