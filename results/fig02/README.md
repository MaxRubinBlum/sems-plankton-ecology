# Figure 2 results

Compact checked outputs for the revised matched transition analysis are versioned directly in this directory. The primary scan uses 252 samples shared by 16S, 18S and the trophic table at ≤650 m, representing 52 independent sampling profiles. Candidate-depth curves use a minimum of 12 spanning profiles; the joint bootstrap retains the conservative 15-profile criterion. `minimum_profile_sensitivity.csv` shows that minimum support values of 10, 12 and 15 recover the same transition optima.

The final figure overlays the full 2018–2026 16S scan as a dashed contextual curve and uses `dcm_peaks_from_ctd.csv` only for CTD-derived DCM background shading.

`Figure2_full_tables.zip` is a frozen snapshot of the complete numerical tables used during the revision, including the full matched scan, long-record 16S scans, pooled and state-specific profile-bootstrap replicates, nuisance-model sensitivity and paired-metadata audit. The authoritative scripts in `reproducibility/fig02/` regenerate the corresponding uncompressed CSV files locally. Figure SVG/PDF/PNG files are likewise regenerated by `make_figure.py` rather than treated as primary analytical inputs.
