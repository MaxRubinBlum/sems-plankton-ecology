# Cross-ocean clade-depth validation — Phase 8

Phase 8 compares the East Mediterranean depth direction of the six Syndiniales clades explicitly described as photic or aphotic in Anderson et al. (2024, BATS; doi:10.1093/ismeco/ycae014).

Pre-specified BATS directions:

- photic: Group II clades 10/11; Group I clades 1 and 5
- aphotic: Group II clades 6 and 7; Group I clade 2

All six show the same depth direction in the East Mediterranean dataset. An exploratory one-sided exact binomial test against 0.5 gives P=0.015625.

This is external qualitative replication of clade-level vertical niches, not a formal meta-analysis and not evidence that the two oceans have identical parasite ecology.
