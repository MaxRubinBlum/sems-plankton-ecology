Figure 3. Seasonal restructuring of vertical taxonomic succession and trophic representation. (a,b) Vertical distributions of abundant and depth-responsive prokaryotic and microbial-eukaryotic taxonomic groups in winter/mixed and summer/stratified conditions. Taxa were selected from the pooled dataset before separating monitoring states and standardized within each taxon across both states; bubble area indicates the maximum median relative abundance attained by each group. The same standardized scale is used in both taxonomic panels. (c) Continuous depth distributions of five 18S-derived trophic components after normalization among the same five selected components used in the Figure 2 trophic-transition analysis. Solid and dashed curves show winter/mixed and summer/stratified local medians, respectively; envelopes show interquartile ranges. Dotted horizontal lines mark the pooled matched-sample transition optima at 110 m for trophic representation and 220 m for both 16S and 18S community composition; monitoring-state-specific optima are shown in Fig. 2c,d. Trait proportions reflect marker-gene composition, not direct process rates.
