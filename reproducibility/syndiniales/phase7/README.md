# Parasite–protist community coupling and boundary independence — Phase 7

Phase 7 asks whether Syndiniales track the broader unicellular-eukaryote ecological architecture, rather than merely sharing the same depth gradient or creating the 18S boundary themselves.

## Community concordance

Syndiniales and non-Syndiniales/non-Metazoa 18S communities are normalized separately. Bray–Curtis PCoA scores are compared by Procrustes correlation. Environmental adjustment removes continuous depth, CTD temperature, salinity, oxygen, fluorescence, season, and station effects before comparison.

Archived results: global raw r=0.892 (n=310); global adjusted r=0.766 (n=249); <=220 m adjusted r=0.750 (n=143); >220 m adjusted r=0.493 (n=106). All permutation P=0.001.

Within the manuscript's established vertical habitats, adjusted concordance remains significant in every habitat with sufficient n, ranging from r=0.493 (>600 m) to r=0.857 at the DCM.

## Boundary independence

The objective microbial-eukaryote boundary remains 220 m after Syndiniales are removed from the 18S table (partial R2=0.2085; with Syndiniales R2=0.2110). Thus the broad eukaryotic boundary is not an artifact of parasite reads.

## Interpretation

Syndiniales track the broader protistan architecture beyond the dominant hydrographic gradient. Procrustes concordance does not demonstrate direct host–parasite interactions.
