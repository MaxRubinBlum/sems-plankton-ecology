# Targeted 99%-OTU-level validation — Phase 5

Phase 5 tests whether taxon-aggregate parasite–protist associations are supported by specific original **99% OTU features** rather than being created only by taxonomic aggregation.

## Provenance

The uploaded `rep-seqs-99.qza` shows that sequences were first generated by `q2-dada2 denoise_paired` and then clustered by `q2-vsearch cluster_features_de_novo` at `perc_identity = 0.99`. The analyzed abundance features are therefore **99% de-novo clusters represented by centroid sequences**, not unclustered ASVs.

## Procedure

1. Map each candidate aggregate feature back to constituent 99% OTUs using the same aggregation logic used for FlashWeave input.
2. Within the 249 complete environmental cases, retain constituent OTUs with prevalence >=2% and >=50 total reads.
3. For broad aggregates, retain OTUs accounting for approximately 90% of reads, capped at 20 OTUs per aggregate.
4. Residualize log1p OTU abundances against the exact metadata matrix supplied to FlashWeave.
5. Test parasite-OTU × partner-OTU residual Spearman correlations where co-presence >=20 samples.
6. Apply one BH-FDR correction across targeted OTU-pair tests; support requires q<0.05 and rho>=0.20.

## Original sensitivity result

The initial targeted set contained 102 testable constituent 99%-OTU pairs; 46 retained positive support after global FDR, and seven of nine aggregate candidates with sufficient constituent-OTU co-presence contained at least one supported pair. These values are retained as provenance for the original candidate selection.

## Corrected candidate-set rerun

After the Phase-4 taxonomy and lineage-specific host-evidence audit, Phase 5 was rerun on the corrected high-priority set. Sixteen aggregate associations were selected (all corrected Tier 1 plus the strongest Tier 2 associations); seven had sufficient constituent-OTU abundance and co-presence for the strict test, and **five of seven contained at least one FDR-supported positive 99%-OTU pair**.

Examples include:

- Group II clade 1 ↔ Dinophyceae: 7/12 constituent pairs supported; maximum residual rho = 0.453.
- Group I clade 5 ↔ Dinophyceae: 12/18 supported; maximum rho = 0.628.
- Group I clade 1 ↔ *Prorocentrum* sp.: 3/3 supported; maximum rho = 0.527.
- Group II clade 7 ↔ OLIGO5 ciliates: 3/3 supported; maximum rho = 0.426.

Failure to obtain a constituent-pair test for an aggregate association is **not negative evidence**: some high-priority pairs, including Group II clade 7 ↔ *Acanthochiasma*, did not meet the deliberately strict OTU-level prevalence/co-presence criteria.

This remains a targeted sensitivity analysis, not an exhaustive search. Because 99% clustering can merge closely related variants, results support sequence-cluster-level recurrence but must not be described as ASV-level validation or direct infection evidence.
