## Supplementary Figure X. Hydrographic and biogeochemical structure of chemistry-matched microbial samples.

Principal component analysis of environmental conditions associated with microbial eukaryotic (18S rRNA gene) samples for which complete hydrographic and chemical measurements were available (n = 45). The analysis was based on standardized temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, fluorescence, nitrate + nitrite (NO₃+NO₂), phosphate (PO₄), silicate, pH and total alkalinity. PC1 and PC2 explained 63.0% and 22.6% of the environmental variation, respectively. Arrows indicate the direction and strength of correlations between individual environmental variables and the two principal components. Points are colored according to vertical habitat and symbols distinguish winter and summer samples. PC1 primarily represents the transition from warm, saline, low-nutrient upper waters toward colder, nutrient-enriched deeper waters, whereas PC2 mainly contrasts dissolved oxygen and alkalinity. This analysis complements the broader CTD-based hydrographic PCA shown in Fig. 1b but is restricted to the subset with complete chemistry measurements.
