# Figure 4 — cross-domain community concordance

Figure 4 tests correspondence between paired 16S and 18S rRNA gene community compositions using Bray–Curtis PCoA configurations and Procrustes/PROTEST analysis.

## Main results

- Whole water column: n = 308 paired samples; Procrustes r = 0.91; permutation P < 0.001.
- Environment- and sampling-adjusted analysis: n = 248 complete cases; Procrustes r = 0.79; permutation P < 0.001.
- Adjustment removes variation associated with log-depth, CTD temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, fluorescence, season and station.

Within-regime Procrustes correlations:

| Regime | n | Unadjusted r | Complete environmental n | Adjusted r |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Surface | 50 | 0.843 | 39 | 0.745 |
| Near-surface | 63 | 0.814 | 51 | 0.650 |
| DCM | 32 | 0.790 | 27 | 0.813 |
| Below DCM | 53 | 0.878 | 42 | 0.769 |
| 300–600 m | 53 | 0.762 | 43 | 0.573 |
| >600 m | 38 | 0.754 | 31 | 0.605 |
| Near-bottom | 19 | 0.809 | 15 | not tested |

All tested adjusted within-regime comparisons had permutation P < 0.001. Near-bottom was not adjusted because only 15 samples had complete environmental data.

For the displayed Procrustes configurations, the first eight positive PCoA axes were retained. Sensitivity analysis showed that the conclusion is robust to axis retention: the adjusted Procrustes correlation stabilized near r = 0.79 from approximately 8–20 axes.

The displayed figure uses open circles for 16S and filled circles for 18S. Panel a colors samples by vertical ecological regime. Only a subset of pair-connecting lines is displayed in panels a and b to reduce visual clutter; all samples contribute to the analyses and reported statistics.

Interpretation should remain at the level of community concordance. These analyses do not establish direct biological interactions between individual prokaryotic and eukaryotic taxa.
